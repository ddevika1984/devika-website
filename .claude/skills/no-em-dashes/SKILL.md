# No em dashes

Never use an em dash (—, `&mdash;`) anywhere in copy written for this site.

When a sentence would naturally take an em dash, rewrite it instead:

- Split it into two separate sentences, or
- Use a comma, colon, or a connecting word ("and", "but") instead.

This applies to every piece of user-facing text on the site, not just one section.
